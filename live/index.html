Questions organizers ask.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Live runs in the web browser on any phone.

How do guests check in?

You share your event QR code. Registered guests scan it on their phones and check in. Group check-in and walk-in registration are coming soon.

Can it carry our branding?

Yes. Your event page uses your logo and colors.

Who can see attendee information?

Attendees choose whether to appear in the event directory. Your organizer team can export the guest list.

Do we need technical staff?

No. If you can work with a spreadsheet, you can run an event, and our team helps with setup.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on your event's size and needs. Request a pilot quote and we'll send one.
